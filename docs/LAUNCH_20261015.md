# CodeWithSolo — October 15 launch acceptance

## Positioning and responsibility
CodeWithSolo is the founder-led public proof and sales channel. Cod3BlackAgency is the contracting business. OPEE MAX is an internal research and delivery system, not a claim of fully certified autonomy. Sell business outcomes, not raw AI prompts, programming languages or pages.

**Core promise:** More than a website. A way for your business to work.

## Offer and commercial operating model
- Introductory one-page Launch Site: existing $500 starting point, with any deposit or payment milestone agreed only after an accepted written scope. Domain, hosting, integrations, extra pages, maintenance and third-party fees are not bundled without express agreement. **Owner must approve the offer before publishing.**
- Business Systems: scoped diagnosis, then a priced implementation covering customer, administrative and permission workflows.
- Fixes & Ongoing Care: bounded issue resolution or separately agreed support; never mandatory or hidden.
- Customer ownership, licensing, access credentials, subscriptions, documentation, administration and acceptance are settled in the proposal, never at the end.

## Responsible AI positioning
Define problem → design workflow → use tools and assistants → integrate → verify → improve. Humans retain authority over commitments, payments, sensitive customer data and production changes. Do not claim autonomous certification, client savings or revenue results without evidence.

## Launch sales workflow
1. Inquiry reaches /api/briefing, with consent, validation, honeypot and delivery confirmation.
2. Resend mails the agency; then attempts customer acknowledgment. Unique reference is shown only if internal delivery is accepted.
3. Operator diagnoses outcome, current process, permissions and minimum viable solution. Contact lead without promising automated actions.
4. Send written proposal and establish milestone payments and acceptance criteria.
5. Track progression: Inquiry → Qualified → Proposed → Accepted → Implemented → Verified → Handed Off → Optional Care.
6. A durable CRM pipeline **is not yet installed**. Launch notifications currently go to the agency inbox.

## Trust and conversion design
Three clear starting offers; one primary CTA; short free inquiry; transparent future costs and ownership; public project links labeled by verified live/demo/private status. Portfolio visual treatments are illustrative rather than actual screenshots. No fabricated testimonials, case-study outcomes or artificial urgency.

## Risk register
- Local static preview does not execute the Vercel serverless endpoint.
- Resend on same account must be configured in deployment environment; do not copy secrets into source control.
- Process-local duplicate suppression is not durable; add persistent rate limiting/Turnstile if abuse requires it.
- Production DNS ownership/routing is not verified despite an active API token and zone read.
- Real design previews and evidence measurements should replace illustrative treatments when available.
