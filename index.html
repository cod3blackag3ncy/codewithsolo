03 / THE QUESTIONS THAT MATTER

Who owns it? Who runs it?

These decisions happen in the proposal, not after you discover an invoice or an admin panel you can't use.

A / OWNERSHIP

Your accounts, your business

We specify domain, hosting, third-party subscriptions, source access, licenses and deliverable ownership. Agreed handoff takes place after final payment.

B / MANAGEMENT

Admin panel only if you need one

Simple brochure pages can use a lightweight update process. Booking, orders and customer records call for an appropriate admin/CRM with roles and permissions.

C / AFTER LAUNCH

Support is a choice

We separate bug fixes, content changes, hosting fees, platform subscriptions and new features. Managed support is priced and agreed separately.
